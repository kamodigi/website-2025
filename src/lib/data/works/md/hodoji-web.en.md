The official website of Hodoji Temple conveys, through modern web design, the challenge of how to put Buddhism into practice in modern Japan. It is designed so that visitors can easily find information such as temple events and directions. Responsive design ensures the site displays well on any device.

## Key features

- Responsive design
- Photo gallery
- Access map (Google Maps integration)
- Contact form

## Development process

The project was developed in the following steps:

1. Requirements and user research: rather than doing anything formal, I talked many times with the monks, the staff, and the people who gather at the temple, and worked out how to convey what this temple wants to do.
2. Design mockups
3. Frontend development with Next.js

## Performance optimization

- Image optimization (WebP format, lazy loading)
- Smaller bundle size through code splitting
- A caching strategy
- Lighthouse scores: Performance 95+, Accessibility 100, SEO 100

## Results

The redesign gave people who learned about Hodoji through various media a front door for information. Many things beyond this site have worked together, and the number of people connected to the temple keeps growing.
